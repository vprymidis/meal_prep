Red Lentil Dhal with Rice
30 minFridge: 2 days▶

Ingredients

  • 200g red split lentils, rinsed
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 400ml coconut milk or 500ml water
  • 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Rice to serve

Instructions

  1. Heat oil, soften onion and garlic for 3 min.
  2. Add lentils, spices, and liquid. Bring to boil, then simmer 20 min.
  3. Lentils break down into a thick, creamy dhal. Add water if too thick.
  4. Serve over rice with a squeeze of lemon. Tastes even better the next day.
Chickpea & Sweet Potato Curry
35 minFridge: 2 days▶

Ingredients

  • 1 tin chickpeas, drained
  • 2 sweet potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 1 tin chopped tomatoes
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 200ml coconut milk
  • 2 tsp mild curry powder
  • Handful of spinach
  • Rice or flatbread to serve

Instructions

  1. Soften onion in oil for 3 min. Add curry powder, cook 1 min.
  2. Add sweet potato, tomatoes, and coconut milk. Simmer 20 min until soft.
  3. Stir in chickpeas and spinach. Cook 5 more min.
  4. Serve with rice or warm flatbread.
Rice Bowl with Tofu, Corn, Peas & Carrots
20 minFridge: 2 days▶

Ingredients

  • 300g firm tofu, cubed
  • 250g rice
  • 100g sweetcorn (frozen or tinned)
  • 100g frozen peas
  • 1 large carrot, diced small
  • 2 tbsp low-salt soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 garlic clove, minced

Instructions

  1. Cook rice according to packet.
  2. Heat sesame oil in a wok. Fry tofu cubes until golden on all sides (5 min).
  3. Add garlic, carrot, peas, and sweetcorn. Stir-fry 3-4 min.
  4. Add soy sauce, toss with rice, and serve.

Salmon & Broccoli Pasta
30 minFridge: 1-2 days▶

Ingredients

  • 2 salmon fillets
  • 300g pasta
  • 200g broccoli florets
  • 100g frozen peas
  • 3 tbsp cream cheese
  • Zest of 1 lemon

Instructions

  1. Cook pasta. Add broccoli and peas for the last 4 min.
  2. Bake or pan-fry salmon (10-12 min).
  3. Drain pasta and veg. Stir in cream cheese and lemon zest.
  4. Flake salmon over pasta and mix gently.
Check salmon carefully for bones. Flake into small pieces. Excellent omega-3 DHA source for brain development.
Fish Pie
60 minFridge: 2 days▶

Ingredients

  • 400g white fish (cod, pollock, or coley)
  • 4 potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 300ml milk
  • 2 tbsp flour
  • Knob of butter
  • 100g frozen peas
  • 50g cheese, grated

Instructions

  1. Boil potatoes 15 min. Mash with butter and splash of milk.
  2. Poach fish in milk for 8 min. Remove fish, keep the milk.
  3. Make white sauce: melt butter, stir in flour, gradually add poaching milk until thick.
  4. Flake fish into oven dish. Add peas and sauce. Top with mash and cheese. Bake at 200°C for 20 min.
Check carefully for bones. Soft, creamy texture is ideal for toddlers. Make two and freeze one.
Cod in Tomato Sauce with Rice
25 minFridge: 1-2 days▶

Ingredients

  • 2 cod fillets (or any white fish)
  • 1 tin chopped tomatoes
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • Rice to serve

Instructions

  1. Cook rice. Soften onion and garlic.
  2. Add tomatoes and paprika. Simmer 10 min.
  3. Nestle fish into the sauce. Cover and cook 10-12 min until fish flakes.
  4. Serve fish and sauce over rice.
Flake fish carefully. Tomato sauce adds vitamin C which helps iron absorption.

Afternoon Snack
Mini Veggie Egg Muffins
20 min (batch)Fridge: 3-4 days▶

Ingredients

  • 6 eggs
  • 50g cheese, grated
  • Diced veg: peppers, courgette, spinach, sweetcorn
  • Splash of milk

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C. Grease a 12-hole muffin tin.
  2. Beat eggs with milk. Stir in veg and cheese.
  3. Pour into muffin tin. Bake 15-18 min until puffed and set.
  4. Cool on a rack. Store in fridge up to 4 days, or freeze 2 months.
Make a batch and use all week. Perfect size for small hands.
Dinner — Eggs
Shakshuka (Baked Eggs in Tomato Sauce)
35 minSauce: 2 days▶

Ingredients

  • 4-6 eggs
  • 1 tin chopped tomatoes
  • 1 tin chickpeas, drained (optional)
  • 1 pepper, diced
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp paprika
  • Handful of spinach
  • Crusty bread to serve

Instructions

  1. Soften onion, pepper, and garlic for 5 min. Add spices, cook 1 min.
  2. Add tomatoes and chickpeas. Simmer 15 min until thick.
  3. Stir in spinach. Make wells, crack in eggs.
  4. Cover and cook 8-10 min until eggs are set. Serve with bread.
Skip chilli for the toddler. Bread fingers for dipping. Store the sauce — cook fresh eggs when reheating.
Vegetable Frittata
25 minFridge: 2 days▶

Ingredients

  • 6 eggs
  • 1 courgette, diced
  • 100g cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 50g cheese, grated
  • 100g frozen peas or sweetcorn
  • Splash of milk

Instructions

  1. Preheat grill. Fry courgette in an ovenproof pan for 3 min.
  2. Beat eggs with milk. Pour over veg, add tomatoes and peas.
  3. Cook on medium 8 min until almost set. Sprinkle cheese.
  4. Grill 3-4 min until golden. Cut into wedges.
Cut into finger-sized wedges. Good hot or cold — perfect for next-day lunch too.
Egg Fried Rice with Vegetables
20 minFridge: 1 day▶

Ingredients

  • 250g cooked rice (ideally leftover, cold)
  • 3 eggs, beaten
  • 100g frozen peas
  • 1 carrot, finely diced
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp low-salt soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil

Instructions

  1. Heat sesame oil in a wok. Fry carrot and peas for 3 min.
  2. Push veg to the side, scramble eggs in the pan.
  3. Add cold rice and soy sauce. Toss on high heat 3-4 min.
  4. Garnish with spring onions.
Use reduced-salt soy sauce. Chop everything small. Ensure rice is piping hot when reheating.

Dinner — Beef (1x per week)
Meatballs in Tomato Sauce with Pasta
45 minFridge: 2 days▶

Ingredients

  • 400g lean beef mince
  • 1 egg
  • 50g breadcrumbs
  • 1 tin chopped tomatoes
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp tomato purée
  • Pasta to serve

Instructions

  1. Mix mince, egg, breadcrumbs. Roll into small balls.
  2. Brown meatballs 5 min. Remove.
  3. Soften onion and garlic. Add tomatoes and purée. Simmer 10 min.
  4. Return meatballs, simmer 20 min. Serve over pasta.
Make meatballs small (half a golf ball). Soft in sauce = easy to eat. Red meat is the best source of easily-absorbed iron. Freeze extras.
Classic Cottage Pie

Ingredients

  • 400g extra-lean beef mince
  • 4 potatoes, peeled and cubed
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced small
  • 1 courgette, diced
  • 100g frozen peas
  • 1 tin chopped tomatoes

Instructions

  2. Brown mince, drain fat. Add onion, carrot, courgette. Cook 5 min.
  3. Add tomatoes and peas. Simmer 15 min.
  4. Transfer to oven dish, top with mash. Bake at 200°C for 15-20 min.
Soft mashed texture is perfect for toddlers. Hidden vegetables. Make two and freeze one.

Lunch & Dinner — Leftovers

Use up what's in the fridge!

Sunday is for finishing leftovers from the week. Reduces waste and gives you a cooking break.

  • Saturday's beef (meatballs, cottage pie, stew) all keep 2 days and reheat perfectly.
  • Cottage pie reheats in the oven (180°C, 20-25 min from fridge).
  • Fish pie or curries reheat on the stove or microwave until piping hot.
  • Frittata is great cold or gently reheated.
  • Any leftover rice dishes — reheat until piping hot throughout.

Afternoon Snack
Fruit & Oat Energy Bites
15 min (no bake)Fridge: 5 days▶

Ingredients

  • 100g rolled oats
  • 3 tbsp smooth peanut or almond butter
  • 2 tbsp honey
  • 50g raisins or chopped dates
  • 1 tbsp desiccated coconut (optional)

Instructions

  1. Mix all ingredients until well combined.
  2. Roll into small balls (12-15). Refrigerate 30 min to firm up.
  3. Store in airtight container in fridge up to 5 days, or freeze 2 months.
Make these on Sunday for the whole week ahead. Use smooth nut butter.
