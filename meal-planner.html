Lunch — Chicken
Chicken Pasta with Peas
20 minFridge: 2 days▶

Ingredients

  • 300g pasta (penne or fusilli)
  • 2 chicken breasts, diced
  • 200g frozen peas
  • 3 tbsp crème fraîche or cream cheese
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Handful of parsley (optional)

Instructions

  1. Cook pasta according to packet. Add peas for the last 3 minutes.
  2. Meanwhile, fry diced chicken in olive oil until cooked through (8-10 min).
  3. Drain pasta and peas. Stir in chicken, crème fraîche, and parsley.
  4. Serve warm. Stores well in the fridge for next day's lunch.
Cut pasta short with scissors. Use mild seasoning only, no salt needed.
Chicken & Vegetable Stir-Fry with Rice
25 minFridge: 1 day▶

Ingredients

  • 1 carrot, cut into thin sticks
  • 1 pepper, sliced
  • 100g broccoli florets
  • 2 tbsp low-salt soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil

Chop vegetables and chicken into small pieces for the toddler. Use reduced-salt soy sauce.
Chicken & Vegetable Soup
30 minFridge: 2 days▶

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 carrots, diced
  • 2 potatoes, cubed
  • 100g sweetcorn
  • 800ml low-salt chicken stock
  • 1 tbsp olive oil

Instructions

  1. Heat oil, soften onion for 3 min. Add carrots and potatoes, cook 2 min.
  2. Add stock and whole chicken breasts. Simmer 15 min until chicken is cooked.
  3. Remove chicken, shred with two forks, return to pot with sweetcorn.
  4. Simmer 5 more min. Serve with crusty bread.
Mash or blend part of the soup for a smoother texture if preferred. Great batch-cook meal.

Dinner — Legumes / Lentils
Red Lentil Dhal with Rice
30 minFridge: 2 days▶

Ingredients

  • 200g red split lentils, rinsed
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 400ml tin coconut milk or 500ml water
  • 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 tbsp rapeseed or olive oil
  • Rice to serve

Instructions

  1. Heat oil, soften onion and garlic for 3 min.
  2. Add lentils, spices, and liquid. Bring to boil, then simmer 20 min, stirring occasionally.
  3. Lentils will break down into a thick, creamy dhal. Add water if too thick.
  4. Serve over rice with a squeeze of lemon.
Naturally soft texture, perfect for toddlers. Skip chilli. Good iron source — serve with vitamin C (lemon or tomato) to boost absorption.
Chickpea Fritters with Yogurt Dip
18 minFridge: 5 days▶

Ingredients

  • 1 x 400g tin chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  • 3 tbsp plain flour
  • 3 tbsp grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 2 tbsp water
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Plain yogurt and cucumber for dipping

Instructions

  1. Place chickpeas in a bowl with flour, Parmesan, cumin, garlic powder, and water.
  2. Mash with a potato masher until mostly smooth (some texture is fine).
  3. Shape into small flat patties, about 1 tbsp each.
  4. Heat olive oil in a pan over medium heat. Cook patties 2–3 min per side until golden.
  5. Serve with yogurt dip and steamed vegetables or salad on the side.
Soft inside, easy to hold — perfect finger food. Spread yogurt thinly for dipping. Store leftovers up to 5 days in the fridge, or freeze.
Quick Chickpea & Spinach Pasta
20 minFridge: 2 days▶

Ingredients

  • 250g pasta (penne or fusilli)
  • 1 x 400g tin chickpeas, drained
  • 1 x 400g tin chopped tomatoes
  • 2 handfuls of spinach
  • 2 tbsp cream cheese
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Grated Parmesan to serve

Instructions

  1. Cook pasta according to packet instructions.
  2. Meanwhile, heat olive oil in a pan. Add garlic, cook 1 min. Add chickpeas and tomatoes.
  3. Simmer 8–10 min. Mash some chickpeas with a fork for a thicker sauce.
  4. Stir in spinach until wilted. Add cream cheese and stir until creamy.
  5. Drain pasta and toss with the sauce. Serve with Parmesan.
Mashed chickpeas make a smooth, mild sauce. Cut pasta short with scissors. Spinach hides well in the tomato sauce.

Dinner — Eggs
Cheesy Veggie Scrambled Eggs on Toast
10 minBest fresh▶

Ingredients

  • 6 eggs
  • 50g cheese, grated (cheddar or mild)
  • 1 small pepper, finely diced
  • 3 mushrooms, finely diced
  • Handful of spinach, chopped
  • Knob of butter
  • Splash of milk
  • 3–4 slices wholemeal bread
  • Cherry tomatoes, halved (to serve)

Instructions

  1. Beat eggs with milk in a bowl.
  2. Melt butter in a non-stick pan over medium heat. Add pepper and mushrooms, cook 2 min until soft.
  3. Pour in eggs. Stir gently with a spatula, folding from the edges.
  4. When eggs are almost set (about 3 min), fold in spinach and cheese. Cook 1 more min.
  5. Toast bread and cut into fingers. Serve eggs on toast with tomatoes on the side.
Soft, creamy texture is easy for toddlers. Cut toast into finger strips. Use mild cheese only. Scrambled eggs are one of the easiest proteins for toddlers to eat.

Instructions

Egg Fried Rice with Vegetables
20 minFridge: 1 day▶

Ingredients

  • 250g cooked rice (ideally leftover, cold)
  • 3 eggs, beaten
  • 100g frozen peas
  • 1 carrot, finely diced
  • 2 spring onions, sliced
  • 1 tbsp low-salt soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil

Instructions

  1. Heat sesame oil in a wok. Fry carrot and peas for 3 min.
  2. Push veg to the side, scramble eggs in the pan.
  3. Add cold rice and soy sauce. Toss everything together on high heat for 3-4 min.
  4. Garnish with spring onions. Serve immediately.
Use reduced-salt soy sauce. Chop everything small. Make sure rice is piping hot when reheating.

Tofu & Vegetable Curry

  • 1 tin chopped tomatoes

  • 100g green beans, trimmed

Chicken Quesadillas
20 minFridge: 1 day▶

Ingredients

  • 2 chicken breasts, diced small
  • 200g tinned sweetcorn, drained
  • 100g cheese, grated (mozzarella or cheddar)
  • 4 large flour tortillas
  • 1 tsp mild paprika
  • 1 tbsp sunflower or olive oil
  • Avocado slices and yogurt to serve

Instructions

  1. Heat half the oil in a pan. Fry diced chicken with paprika for 5–6 min until cooked through. Set aside.
  2. Mix chicken with sweetcorn in a bowl.
  3. Place one tortilla in the pan. Sprinkle cheese, add chicken mixture, more cheese, top with second tortilla.
  4. Cook 2–3 min per side until golden and cheese melts. Press down gently with a spatula.
  5. Cut into small triangles. Serve with avocado and yogurt for dipping.
Cut into small triangles for little hands. The melted cheese holds everything together, making it easy finger food. Use mild seasoning only.

Dinner — Beef
Sticky Beef Mince Stir-Fry with Rice
15 minFridge: 1 day▶

Ingredients

  • 400g lean beef mince
  • 1 tsp sunflower oil
  • 2 tsp garlic (minced or paste)
  • 2 tsp ginger paste
  • 150g green beans, trimmed and halved
  • 1 tbsp cornflour
  • 2 tbsp low-salt soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 100ml water
  • Rice to serve (microwave packet for speed)

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a large pan. Brown mince 5–7 min, breaking it up.
  2. Add garlic, ginger, and green beans. Cook 4–5 min.
  3. Mix cornflour with soy sauce until smooth, then add honey and water.
  4. Pour sauce over mince, increase heat. Let bubble 2–3 min until glossy and thick.
  5. Serve over rice.
Mince is easy for toddlers to chew. Cut green beans into small pieces. Use reduced-salt soy sauce. The sticky sauce coats everything, making it easy to eat with fingers or a spoon.

Dinner — Fish
Pan-Fried Cod with Lemon Butter & Smashed Peas
15 minBest fresh▶

Ingredients

  • 2 cod fillets (or any white fish — pollock, coley)
  • 200g frozen peas
  • 1 tbsp butter + 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • Juice of 1/2 lemon
  • Handful of parsley (optional)
  • Bread or baby potatoes to serve

Instructions

  1. Pat cod dry with kitchen paper. Season lightly.
  2. Heat butter and olive oil in a non-stick pan over medium-high heat.
  3. Cook cod 3–4 min per side until golden and flakes easily. Add garlic and lemon juice in the last minute.
  4. Meanwhile, boil peas 3 min. Drain and lightly smash with a fork and a knob of butter.
  5. Serve cod on smashed peas with bread or potatoes. Spoon pan juices over the fish.
Flake fish carefully, checking for bones. Cod is very mild — great for toddlers. Smashed peas are an easy texture for small mouths.

Lunch & Dinner — Leftovers

Use up what's in the fridge!

Sunday is for finishing leftovers from the week. This reduces waste and gives you a cooking break. Here are some tips:

  • Stews, dhal, and curries taste even better the next day. Reheat until piping hot throughout.
  • Cottage pie and fish pie reheat perfectly in the oven (180°C, 20-25 min from fridge).
  • Pasta sauces (lentil bolognese, meatball sauce) can be reheated and served over fresh pasta.
  • Frittata and quiches are great cold or gently reheated.
  • Soups reheat on the stove — add a splash of water if too thick.
  • Batch-cooked snacks (egg muffins, pancakes, energy bites) should still be good.

Safety: Reheat leftovers until piping hot (70°C+). Use within 2 days of cooking. Don't reheat more than once.
